# Supreme Dungeon I — v1.0.0 (first public release)

A complete graphical edition of the terminal roguelike: 60 hand-wired rooms, turn-based battles, 6 mages, the Supreme Mage with 20 attacks, a boss, a shop, mimics, riddles — and 3 endings.

## Highlights

- Cinematic chest opening, battle animations (lunges, projectiles, damage numbers, particles)
- Animated hero walk cycle, detailed dungeon rooms with torches, banners and gates
- ESC pause menu with resume / exit to title
- Full English localization
- 42 automated gameplay tests, all passing
